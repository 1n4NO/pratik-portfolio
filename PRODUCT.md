# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leaders, founders, and design or product partners evaluating Pratik Singh for senior frontend architecture and product-engineering work.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio makes Pratik's work, technical judgment, and product thinking easy to evaluate. Success means a visitor can quickly understand his range, inspect substantial case studies, and start a relevant conversation.

## Positioning

The work sits between frontend architecture and product craft: complex systems are made legible through thoughtful interfaces, durable design systems, and pragmatic delivery decisions.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically scan the homepage, compare selected projects, open a case study, review expertise or writing, and use the resume, email, LinkedIn, or GitHub links for deeper evaluation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js portfolio with project case studies, writing, expertise, downloads, search, and a voice-assisted portfolio navigator.
- Content is driven by the records in `data/` and existing media in `public/`.
- Existing fonts must remain unchanged.
- All text uses normal letter spacing. Titles use 1.2 line height; other text uses 1.5.
- Contact is currently handled through direct links; the archived contact API must not be represented as active.

## Brand Commitments

The name Pratik Singh, the 1N4N0 mark, factual project copy, project imagery, resume, and existing social links are durable assets. The redesigned visual direction takes inspiration from the Piñt Webflow portfolio template.

## Evidence on Hand

Six detailed project records and their media live in `data/projects.ts` and `public/projects/`. Profile facts live in `data/profile.ts`, long-form writing in `data/posts.ts`, and the current resume in `public/resume/Pratik_Singh_Resume_2026.pdf`. No testimonials or commercial performance claims should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the work, not generic capability claims.
- Make technical depth legible without turning the portfolio into a dashboard.
- Keep navigation and contact paths obvious.
- Use real project artifacts as proof.
- Preserve accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion behavior.
